Care Guide

Keeping your bouquet looking its best

  • Keep away from excessive moisture and direct water contact.
  • Dust gently with a soft, dry brush or a light hairdryer setting on cool.
  • Avoid crushing or bending the stems too sharply.
  • Store carefully when not on display, ideally upright.

With basic care, your bouquet is designed to be long-lasting and enjoyed for a very long time.
